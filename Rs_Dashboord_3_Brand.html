How to read
P&L SUMMARY BY TIME VIEW ($K)
$K
Figures are in thousands of US dollars. Month is shown against plan and against forecast; QTD, YTD and ROY against plan; FY is the latest forecast against plan. All periods are read together in one table.
PL, FC
PL = Plan, FC = Forecast. Var $ / Var % vs PL is variance to plan and vs FC is variance to forecast.
YoY $ / YoY %
Change versus the same period last year (Month vs same month, QTD, YTD and ROY vs the same period, FY vs last year actual). $ is derived from the YoY % and shown for dollar and count lines only.
% of FY plan achieved
YTD actual ÷ full-year plan (25% achieved means 75% still to deliver).
ROY
Full year less YTD, measured against the full-year forecast submitted to corporate, not the Hyperion rest-of-year member (which follows the working forecast and changes each month).
