Item 7. Management’s discussion and analysis

Overview

Total revenues for the period were $6,161.2 million, an increase of $469.7 million, or 8.3%, compared with $5,691.5 million in the prior fiscal year. Employer Services revenues were $4,752.9 million (up 8.2%) and PEO Services revenues were $1,333.9 million (up 8.8%). Revenues include $179.0 million of interest on client funds, a decrease of 3.6% from $185.6 million. Excluding client funds interest, revenues increased 8.7%.

Total costs and expenses were $4,781.2 million, an increase of 6.9%, which is below the rate of revenue growth. Adjusted EBIT was $1,380.0 million, an increase of $163.0 million, or 13.4%, and Adjusted EBIT margin expanded 102 basis points to 22.4%.

Results of operations

Employer Services Adjusted EBIT increased 11.4% to $1,416.4 million, and its margin increased 86 basis points to 29.8%. PEO Services Adjusted EBIT increased 16.1% to $184.2 million, and its margin increased 87 basis points to 13.8%. Non-distributed expense of $222.4 million, which is deducted in arriving at consolidated Adjusted EBIT, increased 3.7%.

New business bookings were $424.8 million in Employer Services and $73.4 million in PEO Services, increases of 8.6% and 9.9%. Starts of $298.1 million exceeded losses of $160.3 million, producing net new business of $137.8 million compared with $115.8 million in the prior fiscal year. Client revenue retention was 91.8% in Employer Services and 89.1% in PEO Services. Average worksite employees were 795 thousand, up 6.6%.
